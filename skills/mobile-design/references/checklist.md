# Checklist before showing a screen

Go through every line. A "no" means fix it first.

**System**
- [ ] Every colour, size, gap and radius comes from the tokens file.
- [ ] One accent. Destructive red only on delete.
- [ ] Radii are only 0, 2 or 6 (or your character's three values).

**Default AI look**
- [ ] No gradient, glass, glow or shadow on dark.
- [ ] No pill buttons or chips.
- [ ] No icons in coloured circles, no crowns, sparkles or emoji.
- [ ] Nothing is centred that could be left-aligned (titles, body, lists).

**Hierarchy**
- [ ] One primary button, at the bottom, in the thumb zone.
- [ ] The content (photo, video, number) is the largest thing on the screen.
- [ ] Three text levels at most; titles use the expressive type, nothing else does.
- [ ] Changing numbers are tabular.

**Readability and touch**
- [ ] Text contrast 4.5:1 or more; nothing below 11 pt.
- [ ] Tap targets 44 × 44 or more.
- [ ] Selection is visible without colour (fill or frame).

**Truth**
- [ ] Real-looking content, no lorem ipsum or grey boxes.
- [ ] Prices and trial terms written in full near the button.
- [ ] For a redesign: every old feature is still reachable.

**Logo test**
- [ ] With the app name covered, the screen still looks like this app and not a template.
